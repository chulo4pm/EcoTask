FAQNEW
Is EcoTask free?

Yes. Volunteering and organizer accounts are free.

How do I become an organizer?

Apply as an organizer and upload a verification document. The admin reviews it, and once approved you can post activities.

How do I get my certificate?

After the activity, the organizer marks attendance and issues certificates. Download yours from your dashboard.

Can I leave an activity after joining?

Yes. You can leave any activity before it happens from your dashboard.
